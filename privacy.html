RISO Proposal Automation

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

RISO Proposal Automation is an internal tool operated by RISO Intelligence for its own use. It connects to one Google account: RISO Intelligence's own Gmail mailbox. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from anyone else's Google account.

Google data the tool accesses

With the consent of the mailbox owner, the tool uses the Gmail API to access messages, attachments, drafts and labels in RISO Intelligence's mailbox.

How that data is used

  • To review proposals RISO Intelligence has sent in the past and keep a reference copy of them.
  • To prepare draft emails for new proposals.
  • To send proposal emails and attachments on RISO Intelligence's behalf.

The data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where the data is kept

The tool runs on a computer controlled by RISO Intelligence. Copies of messages and attachments, and the credentials that give the tool access to the mailbox, are stored on that computer. RISO Intelligence can delete those copies at any time.

Sharing

Mailbox content is not shared with other parties, with one exception: to help draft proposals, relevant content may be processed by AI assistant services that RISO Intelligence uses for that purpose.

Withdrawing access

The mailbox owner can remove the tool's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical information such as visitor IP addresses.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to RISO Intelligence through risointelligence.in.
