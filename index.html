RISO Proposal Automation

RISO Proposal Automation is an internal tool built and used by RISO Intelligence, a consultancy working with development-sector organisations. It helps our team prepare consultancy proposals and send them from our own mailbox.

What it does

  • Reads past proposal correspondence in RISO Intelligence's own Gmail mailbox, so that new proposals follow the way we have written and submitted earlier ones.
  • Creates draft emails for new proposals.
  • Sends proposal emails and their PDF attachments from that mailbox.

Who can use it

Only RISO Intelligence. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not meant to be connected to anyone else's Google account.
